## Description (4000 chars max)
Chess Board Duel turns your Chessnut electronic chess board into a complete over-the-board game station for two players — a chess clock, a referee, and a scorekeeper in one app.

IMPORTANT: This app requires a Chessnut electronic chess board (such as the Chessnut Air+) connected over Bluetooth. It is not a play-against-the-computer chess app.

Set your phone next to the board, connect, and just play. The app watches every move directly from the board's sensors — no tapping, no manual input.

CHESS CLOCK, HANDS-FREE
The clock switches automatically when you make your move on the physical board. Fischer increment supported, with presets for blitz, rapid, and classical time controls. No more slapping a clock — your move IS the clock press.

A REFEREE ON EVERY MOVE
Every move is validated against the rules of chess in real time. Slip an illegal move — or knock a piece to the wrong square — and the board's LEDs light up the exact squares to fix. The clock won't switch until the position is legal again.

EVERY GAME RECORDED
Moves are saved automatically as you play. Export any game as a standard PGN file, compatible with chess.com, Lichess, and every major chess tool. Your games are never lost — even if the app is interrupted mid-game.

POST-GAME REVIEW WITH STOCKFISH
Replay any game move by move with a full engine analysis running on your device: evaluation bar, accuracy scores for both players, and per-move judgments that flag brilliant moves, mistakes, and blunders. Find out exactly where the game turned.

PLAYERS & RIVALRIES
Create profiles for everyone who plays on your board. Track head-to-head records, win rates, and accuracy trends over time. Settle once and for all who's the strongest player in the house.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No sign-up. No cloud. Every game, player, and analysis lives only on your device, and the engine runs entirely offline.

Compatible with Chessnut electronic chess boards over Bluetooth LE.

## App Review Notes (for the "Notes" field in App Review Information)
This app is a companion for the Chessnut line of electronic chess boards (physical hardware, connected via Bluetooth LE). Reviewers without the hardware can still navigate the full app: game history, player profiles, game review/analysis, and settings all work without a board. Only live game tracking requires the physical board. A demo video showing the app working with the board is available on request.

The app uses Bluetooth (Core Bluetooth, central role) solely to connect to the chess board. No data leaves the device; there are no accounts, no analytics, and no network features. The bundled Stockfish engine runs entirely offline.

## App Privacy (Privacy section in App Store Connect)
Data collected: None. All data (games, players, analysis) is stored locally in SQLite on device. Answer "No" to all data-collection questions.
