Frequently asked

Do I need a Chessnut board?
Yes — live game tracking requires a Chessnut electronic chess board (e.g. Chessnut Air+) connected over Bluetooth. Game history, player stats, and game review work without one.

The app can't find my board.
Make sure the board is powered on, Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone, and the board isn't already connected to another app (like the official Chessnut app). Only one app can hold the connection at a time.

Where are my games stored?
Entirely on your device. You can export any game as a PGN file from its detail page.
