Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Chess Board Duel stores all data locally on your device and collects nothing.

Data collection

The app collects no personal data, no usage analytics, and no diagnostics. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no third-party services.

Data storage

Games, player profiles, and engine analysis are stored in a local database on your device only. They are never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth solely to communicate with your Chessnut electronic chess board. No Bluetooth data is stored beyond the current session or shared with anyone.

Network

The app makes no network requests. The bundled Stockfish chess engine runs entirely offline on your device.
